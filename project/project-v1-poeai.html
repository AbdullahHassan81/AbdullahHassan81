Scoring logic (v1 — rules only)
This MVP uses simple, transparent rules:
  • Credibility starts at a baseline and is adjusted by caller tag, historical reliability, and consistency signals.
  • Severity is derived from incident type or your optional override.
  • Recommendation combines credibility + severity + immediate danger indicator.
